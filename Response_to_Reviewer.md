# Response to the reviewer

Manuscript: *Thermal Preview Learning and Lyapunov Safeguarding for Satellite Appendage Vibration Control*

The revision treats the contribution as an evaluated thermoelastic integration of established preview and Lyapunov ideas. All added results are simulations; no hardware or flight experiment is claimed. The original submission is preserved in a separate folder.

The new spatial case exposes a failure of the original two-mean-temperature reference. It leaves a millimetre-scale offset despite scalar preview. A separate field-aware moving reference and frozen residual-forcing correction markedly reduces that offset. This result motivates an integration strategy requiring field information; it is not described as generalization of the original six-input neural network.

The extended modal study also exposes sampled instability at 65 elastic modes at 6400 updates/s. That result is included. Finer intervals are tested and the unstable setting is excluded from convergence claims. Failed sensing and residual-delay cases are likewise preserved.

All displayed formulas in the revised Word file are native editable Office Math equations. Numerical formulas, native metrics and the final document are subject to the saved structural and rendering audits. The audit files document completed verification; they are not substituted for experimental validation.
